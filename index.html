Welcome to The Distiller's Estate

Built for everyone showing up at once

Two stories of brick on a hill outside Bardstown, remodeled top to bottom, and big enough that nobody has to draw straws for a bed. Five bedrooms sleep ten. Four of them are king. One of those kings is on the main floor, which the grandparents will find before you do.

The converted garage downstairs is where the night usually goes sideways: pool table, shuffleboard, a basketball arcade, and a few classic cabinets. Out back there is a hot tub, a grill, and a fire pit with ten chairs under string lights, with pasture running off in every direction. Mornings are quiet out here. Bring good coffee.
